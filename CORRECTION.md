# Correction notice (2026-11-01)

**Author:** Francisco Molina-Burgos, Avermex Research Division

This notice supersedes parts of release v1.0.0 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18408176).

## 1. Cosmological scale: withdrawn

Section "Cosmological Scale: Möbius Topology" of the paper cites the CMB antipodal anti-correlation (Z = -6.08) and the CMB x LIGO cross-correlation (Z = 4.31) from Universo-Mobius v1.0.0. Neither survives re-analysis:
- antipodal: p = 0.04-0.10 against isotropic LCDM simulations, and it is the known parity asymmetry, not a topological signature;
- CMB x LIGO: invalid method;
- the January 2026 O4 predictions failed a blind test on GWTC-5.0.

See the correction notice of Universo-Mobius (https://github.com/Yatrogenesis/Universo-Mobius/blob/main/CORRECTION.md). These results must not be cited as support for PIT.

## 2. Galactic scale: the a0 relation is not a derivation

- a0 = c H0 / (2 pi) is a numerical coincidence noted since the original MOND papers (Milgrom 1983). In the paper it follows from dimensional analysis (L/T^2 with L = c/(2 pi H0) and T = 1/H0); the factor 2 pi is chosen, not derived.
- With Planck H0 = 67.4 km/s/Mpc, c H0 / 2 pi = 1.04e-10 m/s^2, against the empirical a0 of about 1.2e-10 m/s^2 (13 % low). With H0 = 73 it gives 1.13e-10.
- It is therefore not independent evidence for topological primacy.

## 3. Not re-examined

The molecular-scale claims (TDA-CUSUM precursor detection) were not part of this audit. Their status is unchanged and they should be judged on their own data.
